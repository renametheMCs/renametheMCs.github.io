The Large and Small Magellanic Clouds (LMC and SMC) are the two most massive satellite galaxies of our Milky Way.

They are currently named after Ferdinand Magellan (Fernão de Magalhães, or Fernando/Hernando de Magallanes), a Portuguese explorer best known for the first circumnavigation of the globe.

However, Magellan was not an astronomer—or even the first person to observe or record these galaxies! The LMC and SMC were known by Indigenous communities around the world for centuries before Magellan ever saw them. We, a group of professional astronomers, believe that it does not make sense to continue naming them after a person who did not discover them.
