What do we want?

Our primary goal is for the astronomical community to update the names of the LMC and SMC. To do this, we are encouraging our fellow astronomers to avoid using the name "Magellanic." We are also planning to propose an IAU resolution, to be voted on in August 2027.

1. Minimize use of the name "Magellanic" in describing astronomical features.

The LMC and SMC

2. Put forth an IAU resolution.

The International Astronomical Union (IAU), the world's largest professional society for astronomy, is also the primary international authority that defines fundamental astronomical constants and gives official designations to celestial bodies and features.

Who are we?

We are a group of professional astronomers, many of whom do scientific research on the LMC and SMC.
